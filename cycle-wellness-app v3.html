What is the Infradian Rhythm?
Unlike men who run on a 24-hour hormonal cycle, women operate on a 28-day infradian rhythm that affects brain function, immune response, metabolism, stress reactivity, and microbiome health — differently in each phase.

The modern world was designed around the male circadian rhythm. When women ignore their infradian rhythm and push through every day the same way, they accumulate hormonal debt — leading to PMS, fatigue, anxiety, and conditions like fibroids.
Why This Changes Everything
Vitti's research shows each cycle phase offers distinct biological advantages:

🌱 Follicular — Rising estrogen boosts problem-solving, creativity and learning. Best time for new projects.

🌟 Ovulatory — Peak verbal fluency, empathy, and charisma. Best time for presentations and important conversations.

🍂 Luteal — Right-brain/left-brain integration improves detail work, editing, and task completion.

🩸 Menstrual — Whole-brain access offers profound intuition and strategic evaluation. Best time for big-picture thinking.
3 Pillars of the Method
1. Food FLO — Rotate foods weekly to support each phase's metabolic and hormonal needs. Not a diet — a cycle.

2. Fitness FLO — Match exercise intensity to hormone levels. Avoid the same workout every day — it raises cortisol and disrupts the infradian rhythm.

3. Time FLO — Schedule creative, social, detailed, and reflective tasks to the phase where your brain is biologically primed for them.
🌙
Sleep & the Infradian Rhythm
How sleep needs shift each phase
Your sleep architecture changes across your cycle. Progesterone (luteal phase) has a sedative effect and disrupts REM sleep. Estrogen (follicular/ovulatory) supports deeper, more restorative sleep.

🌱 Follicular & 🌟 Ovulatory: Sleep quality is best — use this window to establish a strong sleep rhythm.
🍂 Luteal: Rising progesterone may cause vivid dreams and lighter sleep. Magnesium glycinate at night helps.
🩸 Menstrual: Sleep quality dips. Prioritise 8–9 hrs. Avoid screens 90 min before bed.
💡 Vitti recommends consistent sleep/wake times throughout the cycle as the single most important habit for infradian rhythm support.

🫧 Hip Opening & Lymph — Why It Matters
Hip openers decompress the inguinal lymph nodes sitting in the inner thigh crease — the primary drainage point for the pelvic organs including the uterus. Tight hip flexors from sitting compress these nodes chronically.

Rebounding is the most effective lymphatic exercise because the change in gravitational force opens and closes every lymph valve in the body simultaneously. Even 10 min daily creates measurable lymph flow.

Legs-Up-the-Wall uses gravity to passively drain lymph from the lower body — zero effort, maximum return.

Why Hydration Matters for Fibroids
💧 Lymphatic drainage — water is the medium for lymph flow, which clears toxins and excess estrogen from pelvic tissue.

💧 Liver detox — adequate hydration supports the liver's ability to metabolise and clear excess estrogen that fuels fibroid growth.

💧 Inflammation — dehydration increases prostaglandins, worsening cramping and pelvic pain.

💧 Bloating — paradoxically, drinking more water reduces water retention common in the luteal phase.
Hydration Boosters by Phase
🌟 Ovulatory — Cucumber water, coconut water, lemon water
🍂 Luteal — Warm herbal teas, ginger water, dandelion root tea (supports liver & reduces bloating)
🩸 Menstrual — Warm water with lemon, raspberry leaf tea, nettle tea (iron + hydration)
🌱 Follicular — Green tea, spearmint tea (balances androgens), infused water with berries
